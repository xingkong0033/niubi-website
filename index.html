Core Features

Smart token mechanics designed for long-term holders

🔥

Automatic Burn

A portion of every transaction tax is sent to the burn address permanently, continuously reducing circulating supply.

💧

Auto Liquidity

Taxes are automatically swapped into BNB and added to the PancakeSwap liquidity pool, deepening market depth and stabilizing price.

🎁

Holder Dividends

Part of the tax is redistributed to all holders in BNB, so holding NB generates passive income proportional to your balance.

🛡️

Anti-Whale

Maximum transaction size is 0.5% and maximum wallet holding is 1.5% of total supply, preventing large holders from manipulating price.

🤖

Anti-Bot

Trade cooldown and launch-period limits protect buyers from sniper bots, front-running and sandwich attacks.

🔒

Liquidity Locked

LP tokens have been sent to the dead address and can never be withdrawn, giving investors permanent liquidity assurance.
